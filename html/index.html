Optionally lock when the Ethernet cable is pulled or Wi-Fi disconnects, so a laptop running on battery is covered as well.
Hard to switch off

Hide the tray icon and UnplugLock keeps running out of sight. Open it again from the Start menu to bring the icon back.

Locking only helps if Windows asks for a password, PIN or Windows Hello. If your account has none, UnplugLock tells you.

Questions

What happens if someone grabs my laptop while it's charging?

To grab a charging laptop they have to pull the charger out first. The moment it drops to battery, UnplugLock locks Windows, so they're stuck at the lock screen instead of inside your email, browser and accounts.

What if my laptop isn't plugged in?

Turn on the network locks. Right-click the tray icon, open Also lock when, and pick Ethernet is unplugged, Wi-Fi disconnects, or both. Then Windows also locks when someone pulls the network cable or carries the laptop out of Wi-Fi range. Both are off by default, because switching Wi-Fi networks yourself locks too.

Do I need a password on my Windows account?

Yes. UnplugLock locks the screen, but if your account has no password, PIN or Windows Hello, anyone can click straight back in. Set one up in Settings → Accounts → Sign-in options.

Does it sound an alarm when the laptop is unplugged?

No. Instead of an alarm it locks Windows, the same as pressing Win + L, so your files and accounts stay protected.

Is it an alternative to a Kensington lock?

Not really, they do different jobs. A Kensington lock physically stops someone walking off with your laptop. UnplugLock can't do that, but if someone does take it, they're stuck at the lock screen instead of going through your photos, emails and accounts. If you've got a cable lock, use both.

Does it keep my files safe if the laptop is stolen?

It keeps whoever took it out of your signed-in session: your email, browser, apps and everything you had open. To protect the files on the drive as well, even if someone takes the drive out, turn on BitLocker or Device encryption in Windows settings. The two work well together.

Does it work on Windows 11 and Windows 10?

Yes. It runs on Windows 11 and on Windows 10 from the April 2018 Update onwards, using the .NET Framework that's already built in. It doesn't need admin rights.

Is UnplugLock free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT licence, with no account, no ads and no internet connection.

Does UnplugLock collect any data?

No. There's no telemetry, analytics or tracking of any kind, and UnplugLock never connects to the internet, so nothing about you or your laptop is ever sent anywhere. Its settings stay on your laptop, and the code is open source if you'd like to check.

Can a thief just close it?

Not easily. With a password set it can't be turned off or exited without the password, and a guard process restarts it within about a second if it's closed. It's a deterrent rather than a guarantee, though: someone with admin rights who knows what they're doing can still stop it, so use a strong Windows password too.
